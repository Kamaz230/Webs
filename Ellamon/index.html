About Our Salon

Ellamon is a premier full-service unisex salon and spa dedicated to providing top-tier beauty experiences. We cater to the whole family with a comprehensive menu that includes professional plaiting, barbing, hair treatments, pedicure & manicure, facials, expert nail tech services, relaxing massages, hair installation, waxing, body scrubs, and intricate henna artistry.

Our team of skilled, experienced stylists and therapists are passionate about their craft, ensuring every client leaves feeling refreshed and confident. We also offer professional training programs for aspiring beauty experts.
